# UNG Cyber Unit

> **Learn. Practice. Defend.**

The **UNG Cyber Unit** is a student cybersecurity group at the **University of North Georgia** in Dahlonega, Georgia. We help students build practical security fundamentals through approachable lessons, hands-on challenges, team activities, and a supportive cyber community.

Whether you are taking your first steps into cybersecurity or preparing for a Capture the Flag (CTF) competition, you can start learning at **[ungcyberunit.org](https://ungcyberunit.org)**.

## What you can learn

Our learning path starts with the concepts every defender and analyst needs:

- The **CIA triad**: confidentiality, integrity, and availability
- Common threats, including malware, phishing, ransomware, and social engineering
- Password security, password managers, and multi-factor authentication
- Networking, encryption, and how data moves across the internet
- Linux command-line fundamentals and practical security tools
- Incident response, ethical hacking, and next steps for continued learning

Lessons are short and beginner-friendly, with knowledge checks along the way. Start with the **[learning pathway](https://ungcyberunit.org/start)** or browse the **[core topics](https://ungcyberunit.org/#topics)**.

## Put your skills to work

When you are ready for a challenge, work through real artifacts and submit your flag:

- **[CTF challenges](https://ungcyberunit.org/challenges)** — practice crypto, OSINT, web exploitation, and analysis
- **[Security resources](https://ungcyberunit.org/resources)** — community-vetted platforms, tools, and references
- **[Quiz Rooms](https://ungcyberunit.org/quiz)** — instructor-led quizzes for classes and practice sessions

## Join the community

The Cyber Unit connects learning with team-building and leadership. Members can take part in meetings, competitions, guest talks, cyber-related challenges, and the unit's promotion path. New members complete **Promotion Week**, and members can advance through Tech and Architect levels by completing C.I.P.H.E.R. projects.

- **[Join the Discord](https://discord.gg/BjU632E7sV)** to connect with the community
- Check **[events](https://ungcyberunit.org/events)** for meetings, competitions, and guest talks
- Follow **[announcements](https://ungcyberunit.org/announcements)** for unit updates
- Learn more **[about the unit](https://ungcyberunit.org/about)**
